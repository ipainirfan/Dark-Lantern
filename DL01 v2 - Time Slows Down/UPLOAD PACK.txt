Fly Near Light Speed and Your Twin Grows Old 😳 #shorts

Description:
Fly close to the speed of light for one year… and come home to a twin who aged ten. This isn't science fiction — it's Einstein's relativity, and it's been measured again and again.

The proof:
🕯️ 1905 – Einstein (a 26-year-old patent clerk) showed light always moves at the same speed — so time itself has to stretch.
⚛️ Muons – particles born ~15 km up should decay within about a kilometre, yet they reach the ground because their time runs slow.
✈️ 1971 – Atomic clocks flown around the world on passenger jets came back out of sync (Hafele–Keating experiment).
🛰️ GPS – satellite clocks drift about 38 microseconds a day; without the correction your map would be off by ~10 km daily.
