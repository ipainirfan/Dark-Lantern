DL17 – THE INVISIBLE LINE (The Wallace Line)
=============================================
Topic: The Wallace Line / Lombok Strait (Indonesia)
Tone: GeoGlobeTales style — Fast, curious, pointing at the map anomaly first, backed by real Ice Age geology & evolutionary biology.
Length: 10 lines, ~155 words, ~45–50 s target (brisk 3.1 w/s pacing).

SCRIPT (voice\lines.json):
1. [HOOK - MAP ANOMALY] Look at this narrow twenty-mile gap of water between Bali and Lombok in Indonesia.
2. [SIDE A - ASIA]       On this side, you have Asian tigers, monkeys, rhinos, and elephants roaming the jungle.
3. [THE PARADOX]        Cross just twenty miles to the other side, and every single Asian mammal completely vanishes.
4. [SIDE B - AUSTRALIA]  Instead, you find tree kangaroos, marsupials, and cockatoos native to Australia.
5. [THE CRAZY DETAIL]   Even birds that could easily fly across refuse to cross this invisible line.
6. [ICE AGE SCIENCE]    Why? During the Ice Age, sea levels dropped over three hundred feet, connecting Asia to Bali.
7. [WALKING TIGERS]     Tigers could literally walk across dry land—until they hit this exact gap.
8. [THE TRENCH]         Because right here lies an ancient oceanic trench over one thousand feet deep that never dried up.
9. [50M YEARS]          It kept Asia and Australia biologically isolated for fifty million years, and the line is still unbroken today.
10. [OUTRO]             Follow Dark Lantern for more truths hiding in the dark.
