Venus is Earth's closest neighbour and almost the same size – so why don't we land there?
🔥 Surface ~465°C – hot enough to melt lead, hotter than Mercury
🌊 Pressure ~90x Earth's – like being 900 m underwater
☁️ Clouds of sulfuric acid
🛰️ In 1982 the Soviet Venera 13 lander sent back colour photos – and lasted 127 minutes. No lander has lasted longer.
And some scientists think Venus may once have had oceans, like Earth…
