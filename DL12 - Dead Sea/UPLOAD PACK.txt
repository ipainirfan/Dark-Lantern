Description:
The Dead Sea is about 10 times saltier than the ocean—making the water so dense you float like a cork. But that buoyancy creates a deadly trap: if you roll onto your stomach, the dense water pushes your legs up and flips your face down so you can't push your feet down to lift your head. Swallowing or inhaling just a few mouthfuls floods your blood with magnesium and calcium and causes severe chemical lung burns.

In a hospital study of 150 Dead Sea near-drownings, 1 in 4 patients with damage in both lungs died.
Float on your back, keep your face dry, and never dive in.
