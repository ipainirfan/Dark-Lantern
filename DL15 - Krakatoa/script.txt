1. [HOOK]    This sound burst sailors' eardrums 64 kilometers away.
2. [SETUP]   In 1883, the Krakatoa volcano in Indonesia blew itself apart, and over 36,000 people died.
3. [DISTANCE] 4,800 kilometers away, on Rodrigues Island, police heard it and thought it was distant cannon fire.
4. [LOUDNESS] 160 kilometers away, a gas gauge recorded a blast equal to 172 decibels. Louder than a jet engine up close.
5. [WAVE]    Then the pressure wave kept going. Barometers around the world caught it circling the Earth at least 3 times.
6. [AFTER]   Two thirds of the island vanished, and ash turned sunsets blood red across the world for months.
7. [TWIST]   In 1927, a new volcano rose in the same spot. Its name means Child of Krakatoa. And it's still erupting.
