Fly Near Light Speed and Your Twin Grows Old 😳 #shorts

Description:
If you flew close to the speed of light for one year, your twin on Earth would age ten. This isn't science fiction — it's Einstein's relativity, and it's been measured.

In 1971, scientists flew atomic clocks around the world on passenger jets (the Hafele–Keating experiment). When they landed, the clocks no longer matched the ones left on the ground. Today, GPS satellites have to correct their clocks by about 38 microseconds every day — or your map would drift around 10 km daily.
