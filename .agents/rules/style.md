# Dark Lantern (@TheDarkLanternn) — Brand & Production Rules

This project workspace is dedicated strictly to **Dark Lantern**, an atmospheric historical mystery and eerie incident documentary channel.

## 1. Core Visual Style: Layered Cut-Paper Craft Diorama
- **Aesthetic**: Authentic layered cut-paper craft diorama illustration. Visible paper fibers, torn cardboard edges, intricate drop shadows, and subtle stop-motion feel.
- **Color Palette**: Deep matte midnight navy (`#070a10` / `#0a0e17`), warm amber glow (`#f59e0b`), cyan accents (`#22d3ee`), crimson danger (`#ef4444`).
- **Lighting**: Moody 19th-century candlelit diorama atmosphere, dramatic top/rim lighting.
- **Strict Prohibitions**: NO photorealistic satellite imagery, NO neon laser HUDs, NO 3D cartoon mascots, NO modern tech overlays.

## 2. Voiceover & Audio Style
- **Voice**: Landon / Ryan deep, measured documentary narrator.
- **Audio Mix**: Dry voiceover + 50Hz sub-bass dark room-tone drone + crisp procedural Foley (tick, sub, pop, whoosh). Zero background music.

## 3. Strict Rules
- 100% verified real facts (USGS, NASA, peer-reviewed records).
- No gore or graphic violence.
- Never publish or upload online; local builds only.
