Description:
In 2013, the tugboat Jascon-4 flipped and sank 30 m down off Nigeria. The cook, Harrison Okene, was trapped in a tiny air pocket for about 60 hours – and survived.
🫧 The upside-down boat trapped air like a cup pushed into water
🌊 At 30 m, water pressure packs air about 4x tighter – more air in the same bubble
☠️ The real danger: the CO2 you breathe out – around 5% you pass out
💔 11 of his 12 crewmates did not survive
🩺 After the rescue he spent about 60 more hours in a decompression chamber
